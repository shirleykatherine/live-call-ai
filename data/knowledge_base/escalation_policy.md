# Escalation Policy

## When to Escalate

### Automatic Escalation Triggers
The following situations require immediate escalation to a Tier 2 agent or supervisor:

1. **Financial Threshold**: Disputes or refund requests involving amounts over $500.
2. **Multiple Failures**: Customer has been contacted 3+ times without resolution.
3. **Legal Threats**: Customer mentions lawyers, consumer protection agencies, or lawsuits.
4. **Security Concerns**: Suspected account breach, fraud, or identity theft.
5. **Health and Safety**: Order involves medical supplies, safety equipment, or food.
6. **Media Threats**: Customer mentions social media, press coverage, or reviews.
7. **VIP Customers**: Premium members with 3+ years of history should receive elevated priority.

### Customer-Requested Escalation
- Honor all customer requests to speak with a supervisor.
- Do not delay or discourage escalation requests.
- Escalation must happen within 2 minutes of customer's request.

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — Senior Agent
- Handles complex billing disputes
- Can authorize up to $100 in goodwill credit
- Can make exceptions to standard policies with justification

### Level 2 — Supervisor
- Handles escalations from Level 1 that are not resolved
- Handles legal threats
- Can authorize refunds up to $1,000
- Can override policy for exceptional circumstances

### Level 3 — Manager
- Final decision authority
- Handles regulatory complaints
- Coordinates with legal team when required

## Escalation Documentation Requirements
When creating an escalation ticket, the agent must document:
- Customer name and ID
- Issue description
- Actions already taken
- Reason for escalation
- Customer's emotional state
- Any commitments made to the customer

## Post-Escalation Process
1. Follow-up call or email within 24 hours.
2. Ensure the escalated issue was resolved.
3. Update the ticket with resolution details.
4. Offer feedback survey to customer.

## De-escalation Before Escalation
Before escalating, attempt to de-escalate:
- Acknowledge the customer's frustration genuinely.
- Offer an immediate concrete action.
- Check if you are authorized to resolve it at your level.
- Use empathetic language.

## Escalation Rate Target
The target escalation rate is under 15% of all support contacts.
High escalation rates trigger coaching and process reviews.
