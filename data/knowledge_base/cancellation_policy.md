# Cancellation Policy

## Order Cancellation

### Cancelling Before Shipment
- Orders can be cancelled **free of charge** at any time before they are shipped.
- Cancellation requests must be submitted promptly as orders move to shipping quickly.
- If cancelled before shipment, a full refund is issued within 3-5 business days.

### Cancelling After Shipment
- Once an order has shipped, it cannot be directly cancelled.
- The customer must:
  1. Refuse the delivery (return to sender), OR
  2. Accept the delivery and initiate a return/refund request.
- Shipping costs are non-refundable for post-shipment cancellations.

## How to Cancel

1. Log in to your account and go to "My Orders."
2. Select the order and click "Cancel Order."
3. Select a cancellation reason.
4. Confirm the cancellation.
5. You will receive an email confirmation.

Alternatively, contact our support team with your order number.

## Subscription Cancellations

### Premium Membership
- Premium membership can be cancelled at any time.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- No partial refunds for unused membership time.
- All Premium benefits remain active until the cancellation date.

### Auto-Replenishment Orders
- Auto-replenishment can be paused or cancelled in account settings.
- Cancel at least 3 days before the next scheduled order.

## Special Circumstances
- Bulk or wholesale orders may be subject to a cancellation fee.
- Custom or personalized orders cannot be cancelled once production begins.
- Pre-orders can be cancelled anytime before the release date.

## Refund Timeline for Cancellations
| Scenario | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Cancelled before shipment | 3-5 business days |
| Return after delivery | 5-7 business days after we receive the item |
| Refused delivery | 7-10 business days |
