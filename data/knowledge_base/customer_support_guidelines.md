# Customer Support Guidelines

## Support Channels
- **Live Phone/Chat Support**: Available Monday–Friday, 9 AM – 8 PM EST
- **Email Support**: support@company.com — responses within 24 business hours
- **Self-Service Portal**: Available 24/7 for order tracking, returns, and account management
- **Premium Support**: Priority queue with a guaranteed response time of under 2 hours

## Support Tiers

### Standard Support
- General queue — average wait time: 5-10 minutes
- Resolution target: within 24 hours for complex issues

### Premium Tier Support
- Priority queue — average wait time: under 2 minutes
- Dedicated support agents
- Resolution target: within 2 hours
- Callback option available

## Agent Guidelines

### Greeting and Verification
1. Always greet the customer by name once identified.
2. Verify identity using: order number OR email address + phone last 4 digits.
3. Do not discuss account details until identity is verified.

### Communication Standards
- Use the customer's name throughout the conversation.
- Use professional but friendly language.
- Avoid technical jargon.
- Summarize the issue back to the customer to confirm understanding.
- Never promise outcomes you are not authorized to fulfill.

### Issue Escalation Triggers
Escalate to a supervisor or Tier 2 agent when:
- Customer explicitly requests escalation.
- The issue involves a transaction over $500.
- Three or more failed resolution attempts.
- Legal threats or complaints about discrimination.
- Account security breach is suspected.
- Customer is extremely distressed or threatening.

### Escalation Process
1. Inform the customer: "I'm going to connect you with a senior specialist."
2. Brief the supervisor on hold before transferring.
3. Stay on the line for the first 30 seconds of supervisor call.
4. Document the reason for escalation in the ticket.

## Compensation Guidelines

### Goodwill Compensation (Agent Authority)
Agents are authorized to offer the following without supervisor approval:
- Store credit up to **$25** for significant inconvenience
- Free shipping on next order
- 10% discount code on next purchase
- Expedited processing of current request

### Supervisor-Level Compensation
Requires supervisor approval:
- Store credit over $25
- Full refund for a previously denied request
- More than 20% discount
- Policy exceptions beyond documented guidelines

## Difficult Customer Protocols

### Frustrated Customer
- Acknowledge feelings first: "I completely understand your frustration."
- Do not be defensive.
- Focus on what you CAN do, not what you can't.
- Offer a concrete next step immediately.

### Angry Customer
- Remain calm and professional throughout.
- Lower your voice (in phone calls) to de-escalate.
- Do not engage in arguments.
- If customer uses abusive language after one warning, escalate.

### Urgent Situations
- Treat any order with health/safety implications as Priority 1.
- Medical device orders: escalate immediately.
- Missing orders over $200 or older than 15 days: escalate.
