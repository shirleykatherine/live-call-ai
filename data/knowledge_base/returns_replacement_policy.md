# Returns and Replacement Policy

## Return Eligibility

Items are eligible for return if:
- The return is initiated within **30 days** of delivery.
- The item is in original, unused condition with original packaging.
- The item is not listed as a non-returnable product.

Premium members have an extended **60-day** return window.

## Return Process

1. Initiate return through account portal or by contacting support.
2. Receive a pre-paid return shipping label (for eligible returns).
3. Package the item securely in original or comparable packaging.
4. Drop off at any authorized carrier location.
5. Refund or exchange is processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the item.

## Return Shipping
- We provide **free return shipping** for:
  - Defective or damaged products
  - Wrong item delivered
  - Products not matching description
- Customer pays return shipping for:
  - Change of mind returns
  - Accidental orders

## Replacements

### When We Offer Replacements
- The product is defective or malfunctioning.
- The product was damaged in transit.
- The wrong item was delivered.
- The product is missing components.

### Replacement Process
1. Customer contacts support and describes the issue.
2. Agent verifies the issue (may request photos or video).
3. Replacement is dispatched within 2-3 business days.
4. Original defective item must be returned using provided label within 14 days.

## Exchange Policy
- Exchanges are available for size, color, or style changes.
- Exchange requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.
- Items exchanged must be in original condition.
- Price differences are charged or refunded accordingly.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Personal hygiene products (opened)
- Perishable goods
- Digital downloads
- Final sale items
- Gift cards

## Defective Product Guarantee
All products carry a **90-day defect guarantee**. If a product fails due to manufacturing defect within 90 days:
- Full replacement at no cost.
- Expedited processing.
- No need to return the defective item for orders under $50.
