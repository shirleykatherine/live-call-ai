# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Options

### Standard Shipping
- Delivery in **5-8 business days**
- Free on orders over $50
- $5.99 flat rate under $50

### Express Shipping
- Delivery in **2-3 business days**
- $12.99 flat rate
- Available for most destinations

### Overnight Shipping
- Next business day delivery
- $24.99 flat rate
- Order must be placed before 2:00 PM EST
- Not available for all destinations

### Free Shipping Eligibility
- All orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping
- Premium tier members get free standard shipping on all orders regardless of amount

## Processing Times
- Orders are processed within **1-2 business days** after payment confirmation.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.
- During peak seasons (Black Friday, holiday period), processing may take **2-3 business days**.

## Delivery Carriers
We use the following carriers:
- FedEx (primary)
- UPS
- DHL (international)
- USPS (for lightweight packages under 1 lb)

## Tracking
- Tracking numbers are emailed within 24 hours of shipment.
- Track your order on the carrier's website or through our order portal.
- Estimated delivery dates are approximate and not guaranteed.

## Delivery Issues

### Late Deliveries
- Standard deliveries delayed more than 10 business days qualify for expedited reshipping at no charge.
- If a package is delayed due to carrier error, contact us for a resolution.

### Lost Packages
- If tracking shows no movement for 5+ business days, we will initiate a carrier investigation.
- Investigation takes 3-7 business days.
- If package is confirmed lost, we will reship the order or issue a full refund.

### Damaged in Transit
- Take photos of the damaged packaging and product immediately.
- Report damage within 48 hours of delivery.
- We will arrange a replacement or refund within 3 business days.

### Wrong Item Delivered
- Contact support within 7 days.
- We will arrange pickup of the wrong item and expedited delivery of the correct item.
- Customer does not pay for return shipping in this case.

## International Shipping
- Currently shipping to USA, Canada, and EU countries.
- International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes.
- Estimated delivery: 7-14 business days.
- Customs fees are the responsibility of the customer.

## Address Changes
- Address changes can only be made before an order ships.
- Once shipped, contact the carrier directly for address correction (may incur a fee).
