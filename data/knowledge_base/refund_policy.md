# Refund Policy

## Overview
Our refund policy is designed to ensure customer satisfaction while maintaining fair business practices.

## Eligibility for Refunds

### Standard Products
- Refund requests must be submitted within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be in original, unused condition with all original packaging.
- Proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt) is required.

### Digital Products
- Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded or accessed.
- Exceptions apply if the product is defective or not as described.

### Sale and Clearance Items
- Sale items are eligible for store credit only, not cash refunds.
- Final sale items marked "non-returnable" are not eligible for refunds.

## Refund Process

1. Customer submits refund request via support or online portal.
2. Agent verifies order details and eligibility.
3. If approved, customer receives refund confirmation via email.
4. Refund is processed within **5-7 business days** to the original payment method.
5. Credit card refunds may take additional 3-5 days to appear, depending on the bank.

## Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued in the following cases:
- Item is returned in a damaged or used condition.
- Only part of an order is returned.
- Restocking fee of up to 15% may apply for certain product categories.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Gift cards and store credit
- Downloadable software after access
- Perishable goods
- Items marked "Final Sale"
- Shipping and handling fees (unless the error was ours)

## Premium Membership Benefits
Premium tier members receive:
- Extended refund window: **60 days** instead of 30 days
- No restocking fees
- Priority refund processing (2-3 business days)

## Damaged or Defective Products
If a product arrives damaged or defective:
- Contact support within **7 days** of delivery.
- Attach photos of the damage.
- A full refund or replacement will be offered at no cost.
- We cover return shipping for defective items.
